      🛡️ General Safety Tips
      • Never share your OTP, PIN, CVV, or bank password with anyone — including people claiming to be bank officials.
      • Verify the recipient's UPI ID or account number before every transfer. Scammers often use IDs that look similar to real ones.
      • Be suspicious of requests for urgent money transfers, especially from unknown contacts.
      • Always use official bank apps or websites. Avoid clicking links in SMS or email that ask you to log in.
      • Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) on all your financial accounts.
      • Regularly monitor your bank and e-wallet statements for unrecognised transactions.
      • Never install remote access apps (like AnyDesk, TeamViewer) at someone else's request for "bank support."
      • Report suspected fraud immediately to your bank and file a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in or call the National Cyber Helpline 1930.
      🚨 Red Flags to Watch
      • Unexpected large transactions you did not initiate
      • Multiple small transactions in quick succession (churning)
      • Requests from strangers for "advance fee" or "processing fee"
      • Cryptocurrency / gift card payment demands
      • Transactions from unusual geographic locations
      • Pressure to act immediately without verification
      📞 What to Do If You Suspect Fraud
      1. Do not complete the transaction.
      2. Call your bank's 24×7 helpline immediately.
      3. Block your card / account via the bank app if needed.
      4. Report on cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930.
      5. Note down all transaction details as evidence.
      6. Inform family / friends if your account was compromised.
      ✅ Safe Transaction Habits
      • Always use official payment apps from trusted stores.
      • Set transaction limits on your bank account.
      • Use separate cards for online shopping.
      • Enable SMS / email alerts for every transaction.
      • Log out of banking sessions after use.
      • Update your passwords regularly and never reuse them.
